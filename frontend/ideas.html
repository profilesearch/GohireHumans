Help define the marketplace

These are example task ideas, not limits. Record your interest or turn any idea into a draft job. Votes are counted in analytics only; nothing is published until you review and submit.

testing

Test a website signup flow

Walk through a signup or checkout flow on a real device and report every point of friction with screenshots.

Suggested starting budget: $25

expert_review

Review AI output for trust issues

Read AI-written copy, replies, or claims and flag anything inaccurate, risky, or off-brand with evidence.

Suggested starting budget: $35

research

Build a sourced lead list

Find a small set of matching companies or people and cite the public source for every row.

Suggested starting budget: $50

phone_calls

Make a phone call and report back

Call a business, ask a scripted question, and return the answer as a short call log.

Suggested starting budget: $25

local_check

Check a local place or take photos

Visit a public location, confirm what is there, and return dated photos and notes.

Suggested starting budget: $40

data_cleanup

Clean up a spreadsheet

Dedupe, normalise, and flag ambiguous rows in a small sheet, with a change log.

Suggested starting budget: $30

research

Compare vendor options

Shortlist three to five vendors against your criteria and explain the trade-offs.

Suggested starting budget: $45

testing

Human review of chatbot responses

Score a batch of chatbot answers against source documents and record what was wrong.

Suggested starting budget: $35

research

Verify source links in AI research

Open every citation in an AI-assisted report and confirm it exists and says what is claimed.

Suggested starting budget: $30
