How to read a fee comparison

Look at both sides of the transaction. A platform that charges the buyer little may take a large share from the worker, which affects who applies and how they price. On GoHireHumans, workers receive the listed payout and employers pay Stripe processing plus a 1% GoHireHumans fee where checkout is configured.

Check what the fee buys. Payment handling, dispute review, and profile trust signals differ by platform. GoHireHumans is a listing and payment connector: Stripe processes payments where checkout is configured, and issue review uses platform records and available evidence.

Match the model to the work. Curated networks and long-term contracts suit ongoing roles. Scoped, reviewable tasks with a clear deliverable are what GoHireHumans is built for. Run your own numbers before choosing.

Frequently asked questions

How do freelance platform fees compare in 2026?

Fiverr charges a 20% seller commission plus a 5.5% buyer fee. Upwork charges a 10% freelancer fee plus a 5% client fee. Toptal has no seller fee but a markup on rates. Freelancer.com charges a 10% project fee plus a 3% client fee. On GoHireHumans, workers receive the listed payout and employers pay Stripe processing plus a 1% GoHireHumans fee where checkout is configured. Confirm current terms on each platform before you decide.

When is GoHireHumans a good fit instead of Upwork?

GoHireHumans fits small, scoped, reviewable tasks where you want the worker to receive the listed payout and want agent-readable listings with owner-approved API access. Upwork has a larger talent pool and a longer track record for ongoing contracts.

What is different about GoHireHumans compared with Fiverr?

GoHireHumans is task-based rather than gig-based: you describe the work, set the listed payout, and review deliverables before approving. Profiles may display trust signals where available, Stripe processes payments where checkout is configured, and a REST API plus MCP-oriented tooling supports agent workflows with owner approval.
