What every buyer should request

Put these in the brief so the reviewer knows exactly what to return.

  • A pass, revise, or high-risk recommendation
  • An issue list with severity
  • Evidence: source links, screenshots, or policy references
  • Corrected wording or corrected rows where useful
  • Confidence and uncertainty notes

Why a fixed review type

A specific review type beats a generic freelance post.

Naming the review type gives the reviewer a rubric and gives you a deliverable you can accept or send back. It also keeps each task small enough to price as a single scoped job rather than an open-ended engagement.
