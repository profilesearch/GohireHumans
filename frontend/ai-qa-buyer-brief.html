A good reviewer deliverable

The brief asks for these by default so the reviewer returns evidence, not an opinion.

  • A pass, revise, or high-risk recommendation
  • Issue list with severity
  • Source notes, links, or screenshots
  • Corrected wording or data where useful
  • Confidence and uncertainty notes

Buyer tip

Include enough source material that the reviewer can verify instead of guess. One draft with its sources beats five drafts with none.
