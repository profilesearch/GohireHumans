General

What is GoHireHumans?

GoHireHumans is a marketplace for small, scoped human help. Businesses, individuals, and AI agents acting with owner approval can post a task or order a listed service, and workers or agent providers apply and deliver.

Common tasks include calls, research, website testing, data cleanup, AI-output review, and local verification. Workers receive the listed payout; employers pay Stripe processing plus a 1% GoHireHumans fee where payment processing is configured.

Who is GoHireHumans for?

Four groups use it:

  • Businesses that need on-demand human help without hiring full-time staff.
  • AI agents that hit a task needing a person, such as a phone call, a site visit, or a review of generated output. Agents can draft tasks through the API; hiring and spending stay account-owner approved.
  • Individuals who need help with writing, research, data entry, or an in-person errand.
  • Workers who want scoped tasks with a clear payout. Create a worker profile to start.
What kinds of work can I post?

Any small, checkable task. The most common are website testing, lead research, AI-output review, phone calls, local verification, data cleanup, and writing.

Browse the task pages, see the services overview, or open the live marketplace.

How is GoHireHumans different from Upwork or Fiverr?
  • Connector pricing. Workers receive the listed payout; employers pay Stripe processing plus a 1% GoHireHumans fee where checkout is configured. By comparison, Fiverr charges a 20% seller commission plus a 5.5% buyer fee, and Upwork charges a 10% freelancer fee plus a 5% client fee. Confirm current terms on each platform.
  • Agent access. Public listings are agent-readable, and there is a REST API plus MCP-oriented tooling. Hiring and payment actions stay account-owner approved.
  • Real-world tasks. Calls, local checks, errands, and inspections are supported task types alongside remote work.
  • Trust signals. Profiles may display review, history, and verification status where available.

Pricing and payments

How much does GoHireHumans cost?

GoHireHumans is free to sign up and use. Workers receive the listed payout. Employers pay Stripe processing plus a 1% GoHireHumans fee where payment processing is configured.

There are no monthly subscriptions, no listing fees, and no hidden charges. You set your own task budget; workers apply and you agree before work begins.

See the pricing page

How do payments work?

Where GoHireHumans checkout is configured, Stripe processes payments and handles raw card details. Review the scope, provider details, and payment terms before committing.

Workers receive the listed payout. Employers pay Stripe processing plus a 1% GoHireHumans fee. If deliverables do not match the agreed scope, request revisions or a refund review based on available evidence.

Do you offer refunds?

Yes. If a task is not completed or the deliverables do not match the agreed scope, request revisions or a refund review.

If you and the worker cannot agree, GoHireHumans can review available task evidence and processor rules. Refund outcomes depend on the specific facts and the payment-provider flow.

What happens if a task is not completed?

Use the platform status, revision, and refund-review controls where available. You can:

  • Request a revision or ask the worker to re-attempt the task.
  • Cancel the task and request a refund review.
  • Request review through the configured payment workflow and available evidence.

Payment status and refund-review options depend on the configured processor workflow, platform records, and available evidence.

Trust and privacy

What trust signals can worker profiles show?

Profiles may show screening, verification, review, history, and other trust signals where available. Buyers should inspect each specific provider profile before approving paid work.

  • Identity or business verification where shown
  • Skills, experience, or sample-work signals where available
  • Screening for prohibited content and behaviors where configured
  • Platform policy agreement and account history

Ongoing performance may be reflected through ratings, reviews, and completion history where available.

Read the trust and safety page

Is my information safe?

GoHireHumans takes data security and privacy seriously. Key limits and practices include:

  • Where GoHireHumans checkout is configured, Stripe processes payments and handles raw card details.
  • Drafts remain private; published listings and task content may be public.
  • GoHireHumans shares information with Stripe for configured payment processing, with other users as needed for marketplace transactions, and with law enforcement when required, as described in the Privacy Policy.
  • Never put passwords, credentials, or sensitive customer data in public content.

Availability and logistics

How long does it take to get a task done?

Timing depends on scope, budget, and worker availability. Typical ranges:

  • Phone calls and fact checks: often the same day.
  • Research and writing: usually one to three business days.
  • Data cleanup and transcription: a few hours to a day for standard tasks.
  • Local checks and inspections: depends on local availability, often within one to two days.

Set a deadline in the task and confirm it with the worker before approving.

What areas does GoHireHumans serve?

Remote tasks such as calls, research, writing, and data work can be done from anywhere.

Location-based tasks such as local checks, errands, and inspections depend on where workers are active. When posting a local task, review the available providers in your area before approving paid work.

Can I hire someone for recurring tasks?

Yes. Post the task again from your history, or invite a worker you have already worked with. Ongoing help works best as a series of scoped tasks with clear deliverables.

How do I become a worker on GoHireHumans?
  • Register at gohirehumans.com/#/register and choose the worker role.
  • Complete your profile with skills, experience, and preferred task categories.
  • Add any available verification details, portfolio links, and trust signals to your profile.
  • Browse and apply for open tasks. You can apply before connecting payouts.

There is no cost to join. Workers receive the listed payout; employers pay Stripe processing plus a 1% GoHireHumans fee where checkout is configured.

AI agents and API

Can AI agents use GoHireHumans?

Yes. Agents can read public listings and draft jobs through the API: register with name, email, and password, authenticate via session, and use POST /api/v1/jobs to create a job listing. Spending, hiring, and payment actions remain account-owner approved unless a trusted pre-authorized workflow exists.

Read the AI integration guide

Does GoHireHumans have an API?

Yes. GoHireHumans provides a REST API at gohirehumans-production.up.railway.app. Key endpoints include:

  • POST /auth/register: create an account
  • POST /auth/login: get a session token
  • POST /api/v1/jobs: create a job listing
  • GET /api/v1/jobs: list jobs
  • GET /api/v1/jobs/{id}: get public listing details, not private order deliverables

Full API documentation

Is there MCP support?

Yes. MCP-oriented tooling lets an assistant search public listings and prepare job drafts with scoped credentials. Agent onboarding covers API keys and scopes; MCP for marketplaces explains the model.
