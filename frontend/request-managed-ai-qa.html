How the pilot works

Four steps, each with a human decision.

  1. Send intake

    Email the AI output, source or context, risk level, deadline, budget range, and desired deliverable.

  2. Human scoping

    GoHireHumans turns the request into a bounded review brief and identifies the right reviewer profile.

  3. Written approval

    You approve the quote and review plan before any reviewer starts or any payment-processing step happens.

  4. Evidence report

    You receive pass, revise, or high-risk status, issue severity, evidence links or screenshots, corrections, and confidence notes.

What to include in your email

The prefilled template asks for each of these.

  • Review type: citation, support reply, RAG, website QA, agent audit, product or content QA, or other.
  • The AI output, page URL, file, transcript, or agent log to review.
  • Source documents, policy, retrieved context, expected behavior, or acceptance criteria.
  • Deadline, rough budget, and whether this is a one-off or a recurring workflow.
  • Required deliverable format: issue log, screenshots, corrected text, spreadsheet, or summary memo.
