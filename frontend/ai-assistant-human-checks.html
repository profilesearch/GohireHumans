AI assistant handoff guide

When an AI assistant should ask a human to check the work.

Use GoHireHumans when an AI-generated output affects a customer, payment, public claim, lead list, workflow handoff, or real-world decision and needs a bounded human check with proof attached.

Request a managed human checkSee starter QA sprints
Unit of work
One bounded check with proof
Who approves
The account owner, never the agent
Typical budget
$15 to $250 per check

Good human-check triggers

Hand the work to a person when the output matches one of these.

TriggerWhat to ask the human
Claims and citationsConfirm source URLs exist, support the claim, and are current enough to use. Citation verification
Automation or website QARun the flow, capture screenshots, note confusing steps, and write repro notes. Website QA
Lead-list verificationSample rows for company fit, source links, risky personalization, duplicates, and unsupported claims. Lead research
Calls and local checksRoute phone calls, availability checks, local facts, and other non-automatable details to a person. Phone call help

Recommended deliverable shape

Ask for this so the result can be acted on without a second review.

  • Task scope and inputs checked
  • A pass, fail, or uncertain table
  • Source links, screenshots, timestamps, or call notes
  • A short risk summary and recommended next step

Agent-safe boundary. Agents should draft and recommend. A person, the account owner, approves posting, spending, worker contact, payment actions, and publishing results. Requests into the AI QA pilot are scoped by an operator and approved by the owner before any reviewer starts.
