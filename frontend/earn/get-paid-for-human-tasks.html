How earning works

Four steps from sign-up to payout.

  1. 1

    Create a free profile

    Add your skills, availability, and one sample or link that shows the kind of work you do.

  2. 2

    Apply to a job or post a service

    Jobs accepting applications list the payout up front. A service is a fixed-price offer employers can order without a job post.

  3. 3

    Deliver the evidence the listing asks for

    Screenshots, a spreadsheet, a call log, or notes. Specific beats long.

  4. 4

    Employer reviews and approves

Write a strong application or a clear listing

Short and specific on both sides gets work moving faster.

If you are applying

  • Name the job and the deliverable you would return.
  • One relevant piece of experience, specific.
  • When you can start and when you would deliver.
  • One example or link, if you have one.

If you are posting

  • Define the output: the exact notes, screenshots, spreadsheet columns, or recording you need.
  • Set boundaries: deadlines, tools, source links, privacy requirements, and anything workers should not do.
  • Make acceptance clear: describe what a complete submission includes so both sides know what success looks like.
