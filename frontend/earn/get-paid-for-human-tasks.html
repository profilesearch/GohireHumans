How earning works

Five steps describe the workflow, not a promise of available work.

  1. 1

    Create a free profile

    Add your skills, availability and a genuine sample you have permission to share.

  2. 2

    Set up payouts

    Finish free Stripe payout setup before applying to jobs; nothing is charged for setup. Set up payouts. Check supported countries and payout eligibility first.

  3. 3

    Apply to a suitable job or list a service

    Apply only to jobs accepting applications whose scope you can meet. Alternatively, publish a fixed-price offer employers can order once your free Stripe payout setup is finished. Neither path guarantees work.

  4. 4

    Deliver the agreed work and evidence

    Agree the output, boundaries, buyer inputs and deadline before starting. Submit the requested notes, files or screenshots without exposing private information.

  5. 5

    Submit work for review

    The employer reviews the submission. See the payments guide for review and payout handling; do not assume submission means money is already in your bank.

What small tasks can I get paid to do online?

These are possible services you could offer, not a list of jobs currently available.

  • Remote: written website or app feedback, online research, spreadsheet cleanup and AI-output review.
  • Communication: agreed admin follow-up or calls, subject to the task's permissions and requirements.
  • Local: permitted photo or location checks that require physical presence. Confirm travel, access, safety and privacy limits before agreeing.

Show the human work you will do, and clearly describe AI use where relevant. Apply only when you can meet the task's actual requirements.

Are there paid tasks available right now?

Open jobs are limited right now. Check jobs accepting applications or the open paid tasks page for current availability. This guide is not a live inventory feed.

A service listing is a separate path: you describe a fixed-price deliverable that a buyer can order once your free Stripe payout setup is finished. It does not reserve work or assign you tasks. A listing may receive no views, enquiries or orders.

Can I do tasks part time or with no experience?

Scoped work can be planned around your availability, but each task has its own skills, deadlines and location requirements. Use a genuine sample to demonstrate what you can deliver; do not claim experience you do not have. There is no promise of entry-level work, regular hours or income. This is task work, not a salaried part-time job.

If you are a student or working internationally, check applicable account, work and Stripe payout eligibility, including supported countries, before relying on the service. Do not pay someone to unlock applications or to buy a promise of work.

Write a strong application or list a fixed-price service

Keep the offer specific enough that the buyer can judge what is included.

If you are applying

  • Name the job and the deliverable you would return.
  • Explain relevant skills with a genuine example or portfolio link.
  • State when you can start and deliver.
  • Ask about unclear scope before committing.

If you are listing a service

  • Title the exact output you will deliver.
  • Define quantity, file format, exclusions and revision limits.
  • List the inputs you need from the buyer.
  • Set a delivery time you can meet and a sustainable fixed price.
  • Include a genuine work sample with permission.

Illustrative service outline, not observed earnings or a live job: “I will return [defined spreadsheet cleanup] with [change log]. Includes [row and column limits]; excludes [new research]. Delivery is [turnaround after buyer inputs] for [fixed price]. I need [source file and instructions].”

For other listing destinations, see where to offer services online. For permission-based promotion, see where to advertise your side gig for free.

Is it free to join, and why must I set up Stripe?

Signup, service listings and Stripe payout setup are free. Finish free Stripe payout setup before applying to jobs; nothing is charged for setup. A free profile alone is not enough to submit applications, and buyers cannot order your service listings until setup is finished. Read the payments guide for supported countries and eligibility.

Workers receive the listed payout. Employers pay a 1% platform fee plus a fixed 3% processing charge where checkout is configured.

How do task approval and payouts work?

Agree the scope before starting, deliver the required work and submit the requested evidence for review. Supported checkout flows use Stripe where checkout is configured. Keep instructions and payment handling on the platform; direct-payment instructions are not allowed in listings or applications.

Review and payout handling are explained in the payments guide. Do not assume immediate bank arrival or a fixed withdrawal timeline. GoHireHumans is not an escrow provider or a guarantor.

How are small contracts different from microtask sites?

GoHireHumans connects buyers and workers for scoped work; it is not an always-stocked microtask queue, survey panel or employer assigning shifts. You choose suitable jobs to apply to or list a service. Availability can be limited, and task requirements vary.

When comparing microtask sites, check how work is assigned, eligibility and qualifications, current supply, payout minimums and withdrawal costs. Do not assume another site's thresholds or schedules apply here. Never share login credentials or accept a request to send money to obtain a task.
