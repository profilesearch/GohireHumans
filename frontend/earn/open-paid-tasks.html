What a strong application says

Employers read dozens of applications. Four short lines get you shortlisted.

  1. 1

    Name the job and the deliverable

    Say which listing you are applying to and what you would hand back: a report, a sheet, a call log.

  2. 2

    One relevant piece of experience

    Specific beats broad. One similar task you completed says more than a skills list.

  3. 3

    Timing

    When you can start and when you would deliver.

  4. 4

    One example or link

    A sample deliverable, a portfolio page, or a short description of comparable work, if you have one.

Keep work and payment on-platform

It protects the record of what was agreed and delivered.

Stay on platform. Do not send private payment details or move work off-platform. Apply directly through GoHireHumans; direct-payment instructions are not allowed in listings or applications.
