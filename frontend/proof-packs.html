What a proof pack contains

Four modules, in order. A good proof pack makes uncertainty visible instead of inventing missing facts.

  1. Scope card

    Buyer, task, inputs, checked surface, explicit out-of-scope items, deadline, acceptance criteria, and data-sensitivity notes.

  2. Evidence log

    Screenshots, source links, row IDs, call notes, timestamps, reproduction steps, and what could not be confirmed.

  3. Issue table

    Severity, location, expected versus actual, buyer impact, recommended fix, and confidence level.

  4. Final recommendation

    Pass, pass with caveats, do not ship, re-run required, or needs a specialist review before buyer use.

How proof packs are judged

Three working rules for every first order.

Acceptance rule

A good proof pack makes uncertainty visible. Workers should not invent missing facts; they should mark unknowns clearly.

Founder review while early

For first orders, GoHireHumans should review deliverables before buyer delivery where feasible.

Case-study capture

After acceptance, ask for testimonial or anonymized case-study permission. Do not imply public usage without approval.

Start with one bounded check

Draft a verification task, review the scope, and expect a proof pack back. Nothing is published or charged until you approve the draft.
