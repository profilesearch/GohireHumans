Rates and assumptions

  • Fiverr: a 20% seller commission plus a 5.5% buyer fee.
  • Upwork: a 10% freelancer fee plus a 5% client fee.
  • Toptal: no seller fee but a markup on rates. The calculators model the markup at 20% of the billed amount on the seller side and show no separate buyer fee.
  • Freelancer.com: a 10% project fee plus a 3% client fee.
  • GoHireHumans: Workers receive the listed payout. Employers pay Stripe processing plus a 1% GoHireHumans fee where checkout is configured.
  • Payment processing is left out for every platform, and each platform can change its published rates, so confirm current terms before you decide.

Frequently asked questions

How do freelance platform fees work?

Freelance platforms charge fees to one or both sides of a transaction. Client fees are added on top of the listed project price; freelancer fees are deducted before payout. GoHireHumans keeps the connector model simple: workers receive the listed payout, while employers pay Stripe processing plus a 1% GoHireHumans fee where payment processing is configured.

Which platform charges the least on a fixed-price project?

By the published standard rates in this table, GoHireHumans: workers receive the listed payout and employers pay Stripe processing plus a 1% GoHireHumans fee where checkout is configured. Upwork, Fiverr, Freelancer.com, and Toptal add larger seller-side fees, client-side fees, or a markup on rates.

Are there hidden fees on GoHireHumans?

No. GoHireHumans charges a transparent 1% GoHireHumans fee plus Stripe processing to the employer where payment processing is configured. There are no freelancer fees, no connection fees, and no subscription tiers. Workers receive the listed payout.
