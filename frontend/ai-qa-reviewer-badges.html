Reviewer quality

AI QA reviewer badges.

The proposed way to qualify reviewers by task type instead of treating every worker as interchangeable: six badges, each earned through sample tasks in that category.

Badges by task type

Each badge maps to one fixed review service.

BadgeWhat the reviewer checks
Fact-check reviewerChecks claims against sources and flags hallucinations, weak evidence, and misleading statements.
Citation verifierChecks whether citations exist, are relevant, and actually support the claim.
Support reply QAReviews customer-facing AI replies for policy fit, tone, correctness, and escalation risk.
RAG groundedness reviewerCompares AI answers with retrieved documents and identifies unsupported statements.
Product listing QAChecks ecommerce listings for accuracy, completeness, risky claims, and clarity.
Agent audit reviewerReviews AI-agent output and available trace or context for whether an action is appropriate.

Calibration standards

How a badge would be earned and kept.

  • A reviewer completes sample tasks in the category before receiving the badge.
  • Reports must include human-written rationale and evidence, not only a score.
  • Repeated low-effort or AI-only reviews lose eligibility.
  • Quality is tracked by category, not only as one global rating.

Status. This is the proposed framework for the managed pilot. Badges are not yet shown on worker profiles, and a badge would describe completed calibration work, not a background check or identity check.
