What an SEO specialist does

An SEO specialist improves how a site is crawled, understood, and ranked by search engines, and turns that into a prioritized list of changes. Audits, keyword maps, and page-level fixes are all easy to scope and check. Work you can post:

  • Technical SEO audits (Core Web Vitals, crawlability, site architecture)
  • Keyword research and content strategy
  • On-page optimization (meta tags, headings, internal linking)
  • Link building and digital PR outreach
  • Local SEO (Google Business Profile, citations, reviews)
  • E-commerce SEO (product page optimization, schema markup)
  • SEO content briefs and optimization of existing pages
  • Competitor analysis and ranking monitoring

What to look for on a profile

Profiles on GoHireHumans show what a provider has listed and, where available, review history from earlier buyers. Before you order or hire, look for:

  • Samples of case studies with before-and-after traffic or ranking data, not only a list of skills.
  • Listings that name the tools they work in, such as Google Search Console, Ahrefs, Semrush, or Screaming Frog.
  • Review history and notes from earlier tasks, where available.
  • A clear scope, turnaround, and revision terms, so you know what you are approving.
  • Realistic timelines. Be cautious of anyone promising specific rankings or overnight results.

Start with a small paid task before committing to ongoing work. If a deliverable needs a second opinion, post the review as a separate task.

Common questions

How much does a freelance SEO specialist cost?

SEO specialists on GoHireHumans typically charge $30 to $100 or more per hour. Basic audits and keyword research are usually $30 to $50 per hour, full strategy and link building $50 to $75, and senior consultants $75 to $100 or more. Monthly retainers often range from $1,000 to $5,000. Workers receive the listed payout; employers pay Stripe processing plus a 1% GoHireHumans fee where checkout is configured.

How long does SEO take to show results?

Most SEO work shows initial improvements in three to six months and clearer results in six to twelve. Technical fixes can show sooner. Be cautious of anyone promising specific rankings or overnight results.

What should I look for when hiring an SEO specialist?

Case studies with before-and-after data, knowledge of recent search engine updates, technical skills, transparent reporting, and realistic expectations. Avoid anyone promising a number-one ranking.

Do I need an SEO specialist or an agency?

Freelance SEO specialists offer more direct attention at $30 to $100 per hour, compared with agencies that often charge $2,000 to $10,000 or more per month. For small and mid-size businesses, a scoped task with a freelancer is usually the better starting point.
