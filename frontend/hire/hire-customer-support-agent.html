What a customer support agent does

A customer support agent answers customers across email, chat, phone, and social channels, resolves routine issues against your policy, and escalates the rest. Support work scopes well by channel, hours, and ticket volume. Work you can post:

  • Email support and ticket management (Zendesk, Freshdesk, Intercom)
  • Live chat support during set hours
  • Phone support and call handling
  • Social media customer service (X, Facebook, and Instagram messages)
  • Order tracking and fulfillment inquiries
  • Returns, refunds, and complaint handling against your policy
  • Knowledge base and FAQ writing
  • Customer onboarding and account setup help

What to look for on a profile

Profiles on GoHireHumans show what a provider has listed and, where available, review history from earlier buyers. Before you order or hire, look for:

  • Samples of the products or industries they have supported and the channels they covered, not only a list of skills.
  • Listings that name the tools they work in, such as Zendesk, Freshdesk, Intercom, or HubSpot.
  • Review history and notes from earlier tasks, where available.
  • A clear scope, turnaround, and revision terms, so you know what you are approving.
  • Availability that matches your hours, including weekends or holidays if you need them.

Start with a small paid task before committing to ongoing work. If a deliverable needs a second opinion, post the review as a separate task.

Common questions

How much does a freelance customer support agent cost?

Freelance customer support agents on GoHireHumans typically charge $15 to $40 per hour. Basic email support is usually $15 to $22, multi-channel support $22 to $32, and senior agents who handle escalations $32 to $40 or more. Workers receive the listed payout; employers pay Stripe processing plus a 1% GoHireHumans fee where checkout is configured.

What tools should a support agent know?

Zendesk, Freshdesk, or Intercom for ticket management; CRM experience with Salesforce or HubSpot; and live chat tools. Phone support requires familiarity with a VoIP system.

Can I hire support agents for weekends and holidays?

Yes. Workers on GoHireHumans are spread across time zones, so you can find agents available for evenings, weekends, and holidays. State the schedule and hours you need in the task.

How do I keep service quality high?

Set clear targets such as first-response time, resolution rate, and customer satisfaction score, provide a knowledge base and a tone guide, start with a trial period, and review a sample of replies before approving each period's work.
