What a human tester gives you

Automated checks tell you whether a page loads. A person tells you whether they understood it, where they hesitated, and which step made them give up. Most small testing tasks fit in 15 to 60 minutes: a first-impression walkthrough, one signup or checkout flow, or a checklist on a phone.

App makers often need testers with a specific device, such as an iPhone on a recent iOS version or a Windows PC, and a short screen recording of the session. Say exactly which device, which build or link, and what you want recorded. Give testers a test account rather than a real customer account.

GoHireHumans is a broad marketplace for small, scoped human help that AI and software cannot reliably do alone; testing is one category.

How to scope a testing task

  • Device and access: name the device, operating system, browser, and app build or link, and provide a test account. Never post passwords or real customer data in a public listing.
  • What to do: list the flow or checklist step by step, and how long the session should take.
  • What to send back: bug list with steps to reproduce, screenshots, a screen recording, or written impressions; say which.
  • When it is finished: describe what a complete submission includes so both sides know when the task is done.

Rules for this kind of task

  • Use test accounts and test payment details; do not ask testers to spend their own money or enter real card numbers.
  • Do not ask testers to bypass CAPTCHAs, rate limits, or other security controls, or to create fake accounts at scale.
  • Do not pay for app store reviews or ratings. Ask for private feedback instead; paid reviews break app store rules.
  • Keep personal data out of screenshots and recordings unless the tester agrees and you need it.

How hiring works on GoHireHumans

  1. Post a scoped task

    Describe one fixed-price deliverable, set the listed payout, and review your draft before publishing. Posting is free.

  2. Review applicants

    Workers must finish Stripe payout setup before they can apply to jobs. Compare samples, their proposed approach, and timeline. Payout setup is a payment prerequisite, not a skills assessment. You do not have to hire an applicant.

  3. Hire with a saved card

    The worker submits files through the order. Check them against your brief and request a revision if the agreed scope is not met.

  5. Approve the work

    When you approve the work, the worker payout is released through the configured Stripe workflow. Bank arrival depends on the payout account and processor. Read the payment and dispute rules before hiring.

Common questions

How much should I pay someone to test my website?

GoHireHumans has no fixed testing rate; you set the payout. Many small testing tasks are 15 to 60 minutes of work, and a short walkthrough is often offered at $10 to $25. Pay more for a specific device, a screen recording, or several flows.

Can I hire someone to test my app on an iPhone or Android phone?

Yes. GoHireHumans lets you post an app testing task that names the device and operating system version you need, how to install the build, and the checklist to follow. Applicants can say which devices they have before you hire.

Can testers send a screen recording?

Yes, if you ask for it in the brief. On GoHireHumans the deliverable is whatever your task describes, so say whether you want a screen recording, screenshots, a bug list, or written notes, and how long the recording should be.

Will testers leave reviews for my app?

No. GoHireHumans testing tasks should ask for private feedback only. Paying for app store reviews or ratings breaks Apple and Google rules, so keep reviews out of the brief.

Do I have to give testers access to my real account?

No. On GoHireHumans, give testers a test account and test payment details instead of a real account. Do not post passwords in a public listing; share access privately after you choose a worker.
