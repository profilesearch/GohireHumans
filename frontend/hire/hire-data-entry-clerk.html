What a data entry clerk does

A data entry clerk moves information from one place to another accurately: forms into spreadsheets, invoices into accounting software, scanned pages into text. The work is easy to scope by batch size and accuracy target. Work you can post:

  • Spreadsheet data entry (Excel, Google Sheets, Airtable)
  • CRM data input and updates (Salesforce, HubSpot)
  • Invoice and receipt processing
  • Database entry and cleanup
  • Document digitization and transcription
  • Web research and data collection
  • Email list management and updates
  • Product listing entry for e-commerce

What to look for on a profile

Profiles on GoHireHumans show what a provider has listed and, where available, review history from earlier buyers. Before you order or hire, look for:

  • Samples of the kinds of records they have entered and typical volumes, not only a list of skills.
  • Listings that name the tools they work in, such as Excel, Google Sheets, Airtable, or your CRM.
  • Review history and notes from earlier tasks, where available.
  • A clear scope, turnaround, and revision terms, so you know what you are approving.
  • A stated accuracy rate and a description of how they check their own work.

Start with a small paid task before committing to ongoing work. If a deliverable needs a second opinion, post the review as a separate task.

Common questions

How much does a data entry clerk cost?

Data entry clerks on GoHireHumans typically charge $12 to $30 per hour. Simple data input is usually $12 to $18 per hour, while specialized database work is $25 to $30 or more. Workers receive the listed payout; employers pay Stripe processing plus a 1% GoHireHumans fee where checkout is configured.

How do I ensure data entry accuracy?

Request a small paid test batch, set an accuracy target of 98% or higher, provide a template and a style guide, and review each batch before approving the next. Ask for unclear entries to be flagged rather than guessed.

What typing speed should I look for?

A minimum of 50 words per minute for basic data entry and 65 or more for high-volume work. Speed matters, but accuracy of 98% or higher matters more than raw speed.

Can I hire data entry clerks for short-term projects?

Yes. GoHireHumans supports both one-time projects and ongoing engagements with no minimum commitment. Post the batch you need and hire for as little or as much work as you have.
