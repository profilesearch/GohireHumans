What a data entry specialist does

Data entry specialists go beyond typing. They manage, clean, validate, and organize data across business systems, often with domain knowledge that keeps the work accurate and compliant. Work you can post:

  • CRM data management (Salesforce, HubSpot, Zoho): imports, deduplication, field mapping
  • Medical records and healthcare data (EHR systems, ICD coding, HIPAA requirements)
  • Financial data processing (invoices, reconciliation, QuickBooks, Xero)
  • Research data compilation and categorization from multiple sources
  • E-commerce catalog management (listings, inventory, descriptions)
  • Document digitization and OCR checking
  • Database migration and cleanup between systems
  • Spreadsheet automation with formulas, macros, and validation rules

Specialist or clerk? Hire a specialist when the work needs domain knowledge (medical coding, financial compliance), tool-specific skills (Salesforce administration, ERP migrations), or cleanup and validation logic. For straightforward typing and input, a data entry clerk is the better fit.

What to look for on a profile

Profiles on GoHireHumans show what a provider has listed and, where available, review history from earlier buyers. Before you order or hire, look for:

  • Samples of the systems and record types they have handled, with volumes, not only a list of skills.
  • Listings that name the tools they work in, such as Salesforce, HubSpot, QuickBooks, SAP, or an EHR system.
  • Review history and notes from earlier tasks, where available.
  • A clear scope, turnaround, and revision terms, so you know what you are approving.
  • Willingness to sign an NDA or BAA before handling regulated data.

Start with a small paid task before committing to ongoing work. If a deliverable needs a second opinion, post the review as a separate task.

Common questions

What is the difference between a data entry clerk and a specialist?

Data entry clerks handle basic typing and input tasks. Specialists bring domain expertise such as medical coding, financial reconciliation, CRM administration, or research data management, and typically deliver higher accuracy with less supervision.

How much does a data entry specialist cost?

Rates on GoHireHumans typically range from $15 to $45 per hour depending on specialization. What tools should a data entry specialist know?

Excel or Google Sheets, Salesforce, HubSpot, QuickBooks, SAP, and industry-specific EHR or ERP systems. The best specialists are also comfortable with data validation, cleanup tools, and automation through macros or scripts.

How do I ensure data accuracy?

Set an accuracy target of 98% or higher, provide templates and a style guide, review each batch before approving the next, and request a small paid test task before committing to a large project.
