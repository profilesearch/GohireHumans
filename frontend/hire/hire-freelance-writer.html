What a freelance writer does

A freelance writer produces written content for businesses and individuals on a per-piece or retainer basis. The most useful briefs name the reader, the goal, and the format. Work you can post:

  • Blog posts and articles written to a brief, with sources for any statistics
  • Website copy: landing pages, about pages, product and service descriptions
  • Technical writing: user manuals, API documentation, knowledge base articles, and SOPs
  • Email marketing: newsletters, drip sequences, onboarding and re-engagement emails
  • SEO content: keyword-researched articles written for a stated target query
  • Ghostwriting: thought-leadership articles, LinkedIn posts, and book chapters
  • Social media copy: captions, scripts, thread copy, and ad text
  • Editing and proofreading of drafts you already have

What to look for on a profile

Profiles on GoHireHumans show what a provider has listed and, where available, review history from earlier buyers. Before you order or hire, look for:

  • Samples of published pieces in a similar format and industry, not only a list of skills.
  • Listings that name the tools they work in, such as Google Docs, WordPress, or your CMS.
  • Review history and notes from earlier tasks, where available.
  • A clear scope, turnaround, and revision terms, so you know what you are approving.

Start with a small paid task before committing to ongoing work. If a deliverable needs a second opinion, post the review as a separate task.

Common questions

How much does it cost to hire a freelance writer?

Rates vary by format and experience. Blog posts typically cost $50 to $300 per piece, website copy $100 to $500 per page, and technical writing $0.10 to $1.00 per word. Hourly rates run from $25 to $150. Workers receive the listed payout; employers pay Stripe processing plus a 1% GoHireHumans fee where checkout is configured.

What is the difference between a content writer and a copywriter?

Content writers create informational or educational pieces such as blog posts, articles, and guides that build authority and attract search traffic. Copywriters write persuasive text such as sales pages, ads, and email campaigns designed to drive a specific action. Many writers do both, but the skills and pricing differ.

How do I find a good freelance writer on GoHireHumans?

Browse writer listings by specialty, read their samples for tone and structure, and check review history where available. Start with a small paid piece, such as one blog post or one landing page, before committing to a larger engagement.

Can I hire a freelance writer for ongoing work?

Yes. You can post one-off pieces or recurring work such as weekly blog posts or a monthly newsletter, and scale up or down as your needs change. There are no minimum commitments.
