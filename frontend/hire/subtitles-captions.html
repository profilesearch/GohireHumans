Why people still hire for captions

Automatic captions get names, slang, and accents wrong, and the timing often drifts. A person can fix the words, break lines so they are easy to read, and match the timing to the speech.

Short-form creators usually want burned-in captions styled for vertical video. Longer videos usually need a separate SRT or VTT file you upload to YouTube or your player. Subtitle translation is a third job: say whether the worker must translate by hand with no machine translation.

How to scope a subtitles task

  • Video and language: length, number of videos, spoken language, and any names or terms to spell correctly.
  • Output: burned-in captions or a separate SRT or VTT file, and the style for burned-in text.
  • Timing: maximum characters per line and how closely captions must follow the speech.
  • Translation: target language, tone, and whether machine translation is allowed.

Rules for this kind of task

  • Only send videos you have the right to share; do not send private recordings of people who have not agreed.
  • If you require a human translation, say so: no machine translation or AI translation.
  • Keep delivery inside the order; do not share download links that expose private footage publicly.

Common questions

How much does it cost to add captions to a short video?

GoHireHumans has no fixed captioning rate; you set the payout. Burned-in captions for a video under a minute are often offered at a few dollars per video, and an SRT file for a 15-minute video at $15 to $40.

What is the difference between burned-in captions and an SRT file?

On GoHireHumans you can ask for either. Burned-in captions are part of the video image and always show; an SRT file is a separate text file with timings that viewers can turn on or off on YouTube or your player.

Can I ask for subtitles translated by a person?

Yes. On GoHireHumans you can require a human translation in the brief, name the target language, and ask applicants for a short sample. Say clearly that machine translation is not allowed.

What should I send the worker?

On GoHireHumans, send the video files or private links after you hire, a list of names and terms, the style for burned-in captions, and any existing captions to fix.

How long does captioning take?

GoHireHumans tasks run on the deadline you set. Set a deadline in the task and ask applicants to confirm it; a batch of short videos or a long video needs more time than a single clip.
