What reviewers provide

Every AI QA task should return these.

  • Specific issue notes tied to a location in the work
  • Source links or screenshots where relevant
  • Human-written reasoning
  • A clear pass, revise, or high-risk recommendation

What buyers provide

A reviewer can only verify against what you supply.

  • The AI output
  • The original source or context if it exists
  • The review type you want
  • Deadline and output format

Not a professional substitute. Human QA can catch errors and improve quality, but it does not replace professional legal, medical, financial, compliance, or safety advice, and no review promises a particular outcome.

Tool use by reviewers

Assistance is allowed; substitution is not.

Reviewers may use tools to assist, but the final review must include human judgment, evidence, and original rationale. AI-only copy-paste reviews are not acceptable and can be sent back.
