Lead research

Lead research with human verification of every row.

Give a person your list and your criteria. They confirm each company and contact still exists, score fit against up to three criteria, and return the spreadsheet with a source and note per row. To build a new list from scratch instead, see the lead research microtask.

Why humans help

Where judgment beats automation for this task.

Human-reviewed lead research beats raw scraped lists when quality matters more than volume.

A person notices the company was acquired, the contact changed roles, and the personalization line your tool wrote is about the wrong product.

How to scope it

Four things to include so the first task is easy to approve.

  1. 1

    One clear goal

    Say what the worker is checking and what counts as wrong. One URL, one document, one list.

  2. 2

    Source material or links

    Attach the exact inputs: the page, the file, the transcript, the criteria.

  3. 3

    Deliverable and deadline

    Name the format you want back (table, notes, screenshots) and when you need it.

  4. 4

    An example of acceptable work

    Link a sample deliverable so the worker knows the bar. Keep the first task small so approval is straightforward.
