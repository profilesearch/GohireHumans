Draft title: Website signup flow QA quick check. Skills tagged: website QA, signup testing, screenshots. Starter budget: $25.

What good looks like

Accept the work when the deliverable has these four things.

  • Every step from landing page to first logged-in screen, with what the tester expected and what happened.
  • A screenshot for each issue, taken on the device where it occurred.
  • Issues ranked: blocks signup, causes confusion, cosmetic.
  • Time to complete and the exact step where the tester hesitated.

Why use a human

What a person catches that a second model pass does not.

Your team knows where the fields are and what the error messages mean. A first-time visitor does not. One outside walk-through finds the missing password rule, the confirmation email that never explains the next step, and the mobile button that wraps.

It costs less than losing the next hundred signups to the same problem.
