Good tasks to post

Three ways to scope this task so it can be priced, completed, and reviewed.

Onboarding walkthrough

Ask a tester to record confusing steps, dead ends, and missing trust signals.

Mobile screenshot review

Get annotated screenshots across device sizes and browsers.

Conversion friction check

Have a human identify unclear CTAs, pricing confusion, or form issues.

Website QA proof pack

Ask for the proof pack when you need something you can hand to a developer without a follow-up call.

  • Pass/fail steps in the order the tester took them.
  • Screenshots and reproducible issue notes.
  • Severity labels and the top fixes before sending more traffic.

What to include in the listing

Three things every good listing states before a worker applies.

  1. 1

    Inputs

    Add the exact URL, document, spreadsheet, prompt output, or source list workers should inspect.

  2. 2

    Deliverable

    Ask for a concise report with screenshots, links, notes, priority labels, or spreadsheet columns.

  3. 3

    Acceptance criteria

    State what complete work looks like, what not to do, and whether private data or off-platform contact is prohibited.
