What good looks like

Accept the work when the deliverable has these four things.

  • Every factual claim marked confirmed, unsupported, or wrong, with the source the reviewer used.
  • Broken, unrelated, or misquoted citations listed with a replacement suggestion.
  • Overconfident wording flagged with a safer rewrite.
  • Uncertainty stated plainly instead of guessed.

Why use a human

What a person catches that a second model pass does not.

Language models produce fluent text with wrong dates, invented studies, and citations that do not say what the text claims. A reviewer who opens every source and reads it in context catches what a second model pass misses.

Keeping the task to one document keeps the price small and the result easy to accept or send back for revision.
