Website testing

Hire someone to test your website end to end.

Get a real user through your landing page, navigation, forms, and checkout on the browsers your customers use, and get back screenshots plus a list of issues ranked by severity. This is the general site test; the signup flow page covers a $25 onboarding-only check and the QA task template covers a structured pass/fail report.

Good tasks to post

Keep each task to one of these so it can be priced and approved quickly.

  • Ask testers to complete one clear flow
  • Collect screenshots and concise notes
  • Find mobile and browser issues
  • Prioritize fixes by severity

Why humans help

Where judgment beats automation for this task.

Website testing works best when the task is bounded: one URL, one target flow, one deliverable.

Automated checks confirm that pages load. A person notices that the pricing table contradicts the FAQ, that the back button loses the cart, and that the contact form says nothing after submit.

How to scope it

Four things to include so the first task is easy to approve.

  1. 1

    One clear goal

    Say what the worker is checking and what counts as wrong. One URL, one document, one list.

  2. 2

    Source material or links

    Attach the exact inputs: the page, the file, the transcript, the criteria.

  3. 3

    Deliverable and deadline

    Name the format you want back (table, notes, screenshots) and when you need it.

  4. 4

    An example of acceptable work

    Link a sample deliverable so the worker knows the bar. Keep the first task small so approval is straightforward.
