Lead research

Post a lead research microtask with human verification.

Use a bounded research task to get a sourced list of buyers, companies, or contacts that match your criteria, 25 rows at a time. This page is for building a list from scratch; to verify or qualify a list you already have, see lead research with human verification.

Good tasks to post

Three ways to scope this task so it can be priced, completed, and reviewed.

Buyer list build

Ask for 25 companies with source URLs, relevance notes, and contact hints.

CRM cleanup

Have a human verify rows, remove duplicates, and tag priority accounts.

Niche market research

Request a short sourced list of communities, agencies, or operators in a segment.

Lead research proof pack

Ask for the proof pack when the list feeds outreach and a bad row costs you a reply.

  • A source URL for every row.
  • Row-level qualification notes and duplicate flags.
  • Unverifiable claims and do-not-contact risks listed before outreach.

What to include in the listing

Three things every good listing states before a worker applies.

  1. 1

    Inputs

    Add the exact URL, document, spreadsheet, prompt output, or source list workers should inspect.

  2. 2

    Deliverable

    Ask for a concise report with screenshots, links, notes, priority labels, or spreadsheet columns.

  3. 3

    Acceptance criteria

    State what complete work looks like, what not to do, and whether private data or off-platform contact is prohibited.
