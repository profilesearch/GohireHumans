Phone calls

Pay someone to make phone calls for you.

Post a task for quote gathering, appointment scheduling, business-hours checks, or short call-based research, and get back a call log with who answered, what they said, and what is still uncertain. For checks that need someone on site, see local verification tasks.

See sample deliverables
Starter budget
$30, editable before you publish
Deliverable
Call log with answers, times, and open questions
Scope
Up to 10 calls with a fixed question list

Good tasks to post

Keep each task to one of these so it can be priced and approved quickly.

  • Confirm availability or pricing
  • Call vendors or local businesses
  • Ask a fixed question list
  • Return notes and call outcomes

Why humans help

Where judgment beats automation for this task.

A short human call can resolve information that websites and AI systems cannot verify cleanly: whether the clinic takes new patients this month, what the real delivery lead time is, or whether the listed number still reaches anyone.

How to scope it

Four things to include so the first task is easy to approve.

  1. 1

    One clear goal

    Say what the worker is checking and what counts as wrong. One URL, one document, one list.

  2. 2

    Source material or links

    Attach the exact inputs: the page, the file, the transcript, the criteria.

  3. 3

    Deliverable and deadline

    Name the format you want back (table, notes, screenshots) and when you need it.

  4. 4

    An example of acceptable work

    Link a sample deliverable so the worker knows the bar. Keep the first task small so approval is straightforward.
