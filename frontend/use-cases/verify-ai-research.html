Research verification

Verify AI research before you act on it.

Have a person compare an AI research deliverable to its sources and confirm the names, titles, company data, and numbers it rests on, returning a correction list. This page checks the findings; source checking for AI-assisted research checks the citations link by link.

See sample deliverables
Starter budget
$40, editable before you publish
Deliverable
Correction list with a source per claim
Best for
Competitor, market, or people research

Good tasks to post

Keep each task to one of these so it can be priced and approved quickly.

  • Compare AI output to source links
  • Confirm names, titles, and company data
  • Mark unsupported claims
  • Return a correction list

Why humans help

Where judgment beats automation for this task.

This is ideal for teams using AI agents for first-pass research but needing a reliable human QA layer.

A reviewer catches the executive who left the company last year, the revenue figure quoted from a different company, and the study that reached the opposite conclusion.

How to scope it

Four things to include so the first task is easy to approve.

  1. 1

    One clear goal

    Say what the worker is checking and what counts as wrong. One URL, one document, one list.

  2. 2

    Source material or links

    Attach the exact inputs: the page, the file, the transcript, the criteria.

  3. 3

    Deliverable and deadline

    Name the format you want back (table, notes, screenshots) and when you need it.

  4. 4

    An example of acceptable work

    Link a sample deliverable so the worker knows the bar. Keep the first task small so approval is straightforward.
