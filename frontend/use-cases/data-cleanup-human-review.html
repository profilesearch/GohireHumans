Data cleanup

Have a human clean and review your spreadsheet.

Post a bounded cleanup task: a person deduplicates, fills gaps, tags rows against your rules, and flags what they could not resolve, returning the sheet plus a change log. For lead lists specifically, see lead research with human verification.

Deliverable
Cleaned sheet, change log, unresolved rows

Up to 500 rows

Good tasks to post

Keep each task to one of these so it can be priced and approved quickly.

  • Deduplicate records
  • Fill missing fields
  • Tag rows against rules
  • Flag uncertain entries

Why humans help

Where judgment beats automation for this task.

Human cleanup is strongest where ambiguity matters and automation needs a second pass: two records that are the same company under different names, a field that is blank because the answer is genuinely unknown, or a tag rule with an exception your script never saw.

How to scope it

Four things to include so the first task is easy to approve.

  1. 1

    One clear goal

    Say what the worker is checking and what counts as wrong. One URL, one document, one list.

  2. 2

    Source material or links

    Attach the exact inputs: the page, the file, the transcript, the criteria.

  3. 3

    Deliverable and deadline

    Name the format you want back (table, notes, screenshots) and when you need it.

  4. 4

    An example of acceptable work

    Link a sample deliverable so the worker knows the bar. Keep the first task small so approval is straightforward.
