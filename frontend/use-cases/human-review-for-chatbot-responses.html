Chatbot QA

Human review for chatbot and agent responses.

Send a sample of transcripts from your chatbot or support agent and get a human read on which replies were inaccurate, off-policy, badly toned, or should have escalated to a person. Built for a batch of real conversations, not a single document.

What good looks like

Accept the work when the deliverable has these four things.

  • Each transcript scored on accuracy, tone, policy fit, and whether it should have escalated.
  • Quoted lines for every problem instead of a general impression.
  • A rewrite for each reply that failed, in your voice.
  • Patterns called out: the question types your bot gets wrong most often.

Why use a human

What a person catches that a second model pass does not.

A support reply can be grammatical, polite, and wrong. Someone who has handled customers knows when a refund question needed a person, when a confident answer contradicted your policy page, and when the tone would have made things worse.

A batch review gives you the failure patterns, not just individual fixes.
