What a strong post includes

Three things a worker needs before saying yes, and you need before approving the result.

  1. Inputs

    URLs, files, public sources, screenshots, AI outputs, logs, or exact prompts to review. Do not share passwords or production credentials.

  2. Deliverable

    Issue table, spreadsheet, screenshots, call log, source list, corrected text, or summary memo.

  3. Acceptance criteria

    Checkable statements that define done: number of items reviewed, proof required, deadline, and what is out of scope.

Questions about hiring for a small task

Where can I hire someone online for a small one-off task?

GoHireHumans is a marketplace where you can post a small, one-off task or browse services for scoped human help. Describe the inputs, deliverable, deadline, and fixed budget, then review applicants or a service before hiring. Phone calls, research, website testing, and data cleanup are examples. Confirm worker availability and scope before committing.

Can I pay someone to make a phone call for me?

You can post a phone-call task on GoHireHumans, such as asking a business about opening hours, prices, or availability. Include the number, questions, permitted disclosures, time zone, and a call-log deliverable. Require truthful representation; do not share passwords or authorize purchases or commitments unless those actions are explicitly in scope.

Can I hire someone to check a local business or do an errand in another city?

You can request a local check or a bounded errand on GoHireHumans. Specify the city, exact location, permitted activity, timing, and evidence such as timestamped photos or a written result. Confirm that a worker can reach the location before hiring; posting a task does not establish local coverage or availability.

Can I hire someone to clean up a spreadsheet?

You can post a spreadsheet-cleanup task on GoHireHumans. Define the row count, columns, duplicate rules, formatting, and how ambiguous values should be flagged. Ask for the cleaned file plus a change log, and provide only data the worker is authorized to use. Review a small sample before agreeing the full scope.

Can I hire a human to test my website for a few hours?

You can request a bounded website QA task on GoHireHumans. Specify the flows, devices, browsers, and testing window, then agree a fixed deliverable and budget rather than assuming hourly hiring. Ask for reproduction steps and screenshots in an issue report. Use test access and prohibit real purchases, messages, or production-data changes unless separately authorized.

How are platform fees structured for workers and employers?

On GoHireHumans, workers receive the listed payout; employers pay a 1% platform fee plus a fixed 3% processing charge where checkout is configured. Review the scope and displayed checkout total before paying. Workers must finish Stripe payout setup before applying to jobs.
