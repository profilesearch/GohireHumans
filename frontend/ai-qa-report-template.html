The five sections

A report with all five can be accepted, sent back, or escalated without a follow-up call.

  1. Summary

    Pass, needs revision, or high risk, a one-sentence reason, and the reviewer's confidence.

  2. Issue log

    For each issue: the type, where it appears, why it matters, and a suggested correction.

  3. Evidence

    Source links, relevant screenshots, claim-level notes, and the quote or excerpt that was checked.

  4. Scores

    Accuracy, completeness, and tone or brand fit on a 1 to 5 scale, plus a low, medium, or high risk rating.

  5. Final recommendation

    Whether it is safe to publish, whether the owner or a professional should review it, and what to fix first.
