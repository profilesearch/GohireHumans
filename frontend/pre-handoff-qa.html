Scope card

One bounded flow, explicit expectations, a human run-through, and a report your team can act on.

You provide

  • A description of one workflow and where its client-facing output lands.
  • Test or sandbox access, or recorded runs with enough detail to inspect; agree on access before starting.
  • Expected behavior and 5–10 realistic test cases, or we draft those cases with you before testing.

The human check

  • Run agreed realistic cases and edge cases against the test setup.
  • Compare routing, notifications, and output fields with your expectations.
  • Mark what passed, failed, or could not be confirmed rather than guessing.

What you receive

  • A case-by-case pass / fail / uncertain table.
  • Screenshots or recorded evidence references and reproducible steps for issues.
  • Top risks, limitations, and a retest recommendation.

Boundaries

  • No production credentials, passwords, or sensitive client data in public task text. Arrange only minimum necessary access through an appropriate private channel after scope review.
  • No regulated or legal assurance, security certification, or guarantee that the workflow is bug-free.
  • Timing is agreed per task; staffing and access are confirmed before work begins.

Reproduction notes

  1. A · Duplicate contact: In the sandbox, submit a North lead with test.one@example.invalid, then submit again with the same address and a changed company. Search the test CRM by email: L-04a and L-04b appear. Capture the two record IDs and the two run IDs.
  2. B · Unrouted region: Submit a lead with region “West” (not in the routing map). Open the created contact and manual-review queue: owner is blank and the queue has no item. Capture the run trace and contact record.

Top three handoff risks

  1. Duplicate submissions can split a client's lead history across contacts.
  2. Unsupported regions can silently strand leads without an owner.
  3. Timeout recovery and email delivery cannot be signed off without retry/alert and provider logs.

Limits of this sample: All records and observations are fictional. The review covers only these eight sandbox cases. It does not prove production deliverability, all region mappings, load behavior, or every integration failure mode. Fix failed cases and supply missing logs, then retest before handoff.
