What good review notes look like

Buyers accept reports that show their work.

Be specific. Quote the issue, explain why it is wrong, unsupported, or risky, link the source if there is one, and suggest a correction. Say what you could not verify instead of guessing. Use the report template so every task returns the same five parts.

Tools. You may use software to assist, but the final review must include your own judgment, evidence, and rationale. AI-only copy-paste reviews are not acceptable on any AI QA task.
