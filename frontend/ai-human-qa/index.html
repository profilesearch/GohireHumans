How the managed pilot works

Four steps from AI output to an accepted review.

  1. Pick the review type

    Use a fixed service above, or generate a brief for the exact AI output you need checked.

  2. Send the request

    Email the AI output, the sources or policy it should match, acceptance criteria, deadline, and budget. No checkout or job is created automatically.

  3. Approve the plan

    An operator returns a scoped review plan, reviewer profile, and quote. Nothing starts until you approve it.

  4. Receive the evidence

    The reviewer returns pass, revise, or high-risk status, issues by severity, source links or screenshots, corrections, and confidence notes.
