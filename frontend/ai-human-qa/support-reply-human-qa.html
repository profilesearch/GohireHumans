Customer support QA

Human review for AI-drafted support replies.

Before AI replies reach customers, hire a human to check each draft for accuracy, tone, policy fit, missing context, and escalation risk.

Build a support QA briefRequest managed review
Suggested budget
$25–$100 per batch
Deliverable
Pass, revise, or escalate per reply with corrected wording
Best for
Support macros, refund and billing replies, sensitive issues

Example tasks you can request

Keep each task to one bounded input so the reviewer can return clear evidence.

  • Check AI-written macros before agents use them with real customers.
  • Flag policy mismatches and unsupported promises in refund or billing replies.
  • Review tone and risk on complaints, delays, cancellations, and account problems.
  • Confirm the reply answers the question the customer actually asked.
  • Mark replies that need a human owner's decision.

What the reviewer returns

Ask for evidence, not an opinion. Accept the work when the report has these parts.

  • Pass, revise, or escalate recommendation for each reply
  • Issue severity and suggested corrected wording
  • Policy or knowledge-base references used
  • Confidence and uncertainty notes
  • Escalation flags when a human owner should decide

Suggested payout ranges

Starting points only. Final budgets should match length, source complexity, turnaround, and reviewer experience.

How to scope it

Four steps from AI output to an accepted review.

  1. Send the replies and the policy

    Include the customer message, the drafted reply, and the policy or knowledge base it should follow.

  2. Say which replies are sensitive

    Refunds, cancellations, and complaints get a stricter bar.

  3. Ask for corrected wording

    A revised reply saves a second round.

  4. Approve the quote

    The operator confirms scope, reviewer, and price before the review starts.

Scope note. Human review reduces risk and creates a review record, but it does not promise perfect accuracy, legal compliance, or a business outcome, and it does not replace professional legal, medical, financial, or compliance advice. GoHireHumans is a listing and payment connector; the reviewer is an independent worker.
