The checklist

In the order a reviewer works through it.

  1. Compare every factual claim against source material

    Names, titles, dates, numbers, links, and citations each get checked, not skimmed.

  2. Mark unsupported claims instead of rewriting them as true

    A claim without a source is labeled unsupported, even if it sounds right.

  3. Check what the output is missing

    Caveats, exceptions, and context the source includes but the output dropped.

  4. Flag tone, policy, and brand-risk issues

    Anything a customer, regulator, or partner would object to is listed with the reason.

  5. Return a correction list with confidence notes

    What to fix first, and what the reviewer could not verify.

What the reviewer returns

Ask for this shape so the result can be acted on.

  • A short report with pass or fail notes per check
  • Specific issues with source links or screenshots
  • A prioritized correction list
  • Confidence notes and anything that could not be checked
