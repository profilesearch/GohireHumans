Human AI QA reviewers

Hire a human to check what AI got wrong.

Post a scoped review task when an AI answer, automation output, website, data cleanup, or agent run needs human judgment before you publish, email, import, or act on it. Reviewers are independent workers who return an evidence-backed report.

What reviewers check

The same categories a careful editor or QA lead would cover.

  • Wrong claims and unsupported citations
  • Broken links and missing context
  • Tone and policy risk in customer-facing text
  • UX bugs in AI-built pages and flows
  • Agent mistakes before an action is taken

What you receive

A structured report you can accept, send back, or escalate.

  • Pass, revise, or high-risk status
  • Issue severity and location
  • Source notes or screenshots
  • Corrected wording or data
  • Uncertainty notes
