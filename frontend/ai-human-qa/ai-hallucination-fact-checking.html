AI fact-checking

Hire a human to catch AI hallucinations.

Post one AI-generated draft and get a human check of its claims, names, numbers, and citations against the sources it should match, returned as an issue list with corrected wording.

Example tasks you can request

Keep each task to one bounded input so the reviewer can return clear evidence.

  • Check whether each citation supports the exact claim it is attached to.
  • Verify business names, people, prices, dates, and statistics.
  • Find missing context that changes the answer.
  • Separate wrong, unsupported, and uncertain statements.
  • Return corrected language where useful.

What the reviewer returns

Ask for evidence, not an opinion. Accept the work when the report has these parts.

  • Every claim marked verified, unsupported, or incorrect, with the source used
  • Broken, unrelated, or misquoted citations listed with a replacement suggestion
  • Overconfident wording flagged with a safer rewrite
  • Reviewer confidence and what could not be checked

Suggested brief text

Please review this AI-generated output against the provided source material. Mark anything incorrect, unsupported, unclear, risky, or missing context. Return a concise issue list and recommended corrections.

How to scope it

Four steps from AI output to an accepted review.

  1. Paste the draft and its sources

    One document per task. Include the links or documents the claims should match.

  2. Name the claims that matter most

    Prices, statistics, quotes, and anything customer-facing get checked first.

  3. Ask for labels and corrections

    Verified, unsupported, or incorrect per claim, plus corrected wording.

  4. Approve the quote

    The operator confirms scope, reviewer, and price before the review starts.

Scope note. Human review reduces risk and creates a review record, but it does not promise perfect accuracy, legal compliance, or a business outcome, and it does not replace professional legal, medical, financial, or compliance advice. GoHireHumans is a listing and payment connector; the reviewer is an independent worker.
