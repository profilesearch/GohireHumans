Side gig guide

Where to advertise your side gig for free

Published March 16, 2026 · GoHireHumans

Last updated: October 26, 2026

To advertise your side gig for free, publish a clear service offer and share it through a channel that fits the buyer and permits promotion. Remote research, design or admin services need a different audience from cleaning, assembly or other local work. Free posting does not mean free transactions, free leads everywhere or a guaranteed first client.

Should I advertise remote services or local side jobs?

Remote: describe the file, research, feedback or other deliverable the buyer receives, the tools you use and any working-language or time-zone requirements. A genuine, permission-cleared sample can show the work without promising an outcome.

Local: state your service area, availability, travel limits, access needs and exclusions. Do not invite buyers to post private addresses in a public thread. A directory or neighborhood group is useful only if it accepts your type of business promotion.

Taskrabbit is an option for eligible local workers, but it is not a free-listing substitute everywhere. Its U.S. Help lists a one-time, nonrefundable US$25 registration fee if registration is processed; beyond that, fees are charged to clients, not Taskers. Paying does not guarantee acceptance. Check local requirements. Taskrabbit says it is not currently onboarding virtual-only Taskers; do not use it as a remote-only advertising plan.

How do I write a side-gig ad?

Copy this template, replace the placeholders and publish only terms you can meet:

I help [type of buyer] get [specific deliverable] for [fixed price], delivered within [turnaround after all inputs arrive]. Includes [quantity, format and revisions]. Excludes [limits]. I need [buyer inputs]. See [genuine work sample] and [service link].

Illustrative remote example, not a completed order or earnings claim: “I clean up a buyer-supplied spreadsheet and return a consistently formatted file plus a change log. We agree the row limit, columns, fixed price and delivery date before starting. Data enrichment and new research are not included.”

Illustrative local example, not a live job: “I assemble a buyer-supplied flat-pack item within my stated service area. We agree the item, access, fixed price and appointment before booking. Wall mounting, electrical work and waste disposal are not included.”

Use a sustainable scoped price, not an arbitrary discount to buy early reviews. Do not invent testimonials, conversion results or experience. Make any use of AI clear when it is relevant to the service.

Where can I share my service link without spamming?

  1. Read the rules first. Check the platform and each community's current self-promotion rules, including link restrictions, designated threads and direct-message rules. Helpful participation is not permission to sell.
  2. Share only where it is relevant and allowed. Use your own professional profile or an approved promotion space. If permission is unclear, ask a moderator before posting.
  3. Ask existing contacts for relevant referrals without pressure. Do not scrape member lists, mass-message strangers or add people to a newsletter without permission.
  4. Keep the offer accurate. Use a real sample with permission, remove client identifiers and update availability when it changes.

A permission-based message to an existing contact could be: “I have availability for [scoped service]. If someone you know needs that, may I send you the details to pass along? No need to share if it is not relevant.” This is a suggested message, not an instruction to send unsolicited outreach.

For LinkedIn, use current Service Pages rather than describing ProFinder as active. LinkedIn ended the former ProFinder Pro matching service beginning May 2024.

How do I test a promotion channel?

Start with one offer and one suitable channel, or compare two channels if you can keep the offer consistent. Pick a review date and record the effort and any spending. This is a practical test, not a claim about how many platforms freelancers normally use.

  • Views: use the channel's available reporting; write “unknown” when it does not provide a count.
  • Qualified enquiries: count people whose needs fit the scope, price and location. Ask how they found the offer without collecting unnecessary personal data.
  • Orders: record actual orders separately from messages or clicks. Do not call interest earnings.
  • Decision: no views may mean the channel does not reach your audience. Views without relevant enquiries are a reason to inspect the scope, sample and price. Enquiries without orders are a reason to review fit and unanswered questions.

Change a clear part of the offer or try a different appropriate channel, then compare the next observation. Do not pay for more reach merely because a free listing has not produced work.

How long does it take to get a first client?

There is no guaranteed timeline, and a listing can receive no orders. Avoid treating a few weeks of posting or months of effort as a path to predictable income. Frequently asked questions

Where can I advertise my side gig for free?

Consider a free GoHireHumans service listing, a free Contra profile or a LinkedIn Service Page. You can also share a relevant offer in communities that explicitly allow promotion. Free publishing does not remove transaction fees or guarantee clients.

How long does it take to get a first client?

There is no guaranteed timeline. A service listing can receive no enquiries or orders. If people view the offer but do not make relevant enquiries, review the scope, work sample, price and channel fit rather than assuming income will follow after a fixed period.

Can I promote services in Reddit or local groups?

Check each community’s current self-promotion rules first, including designated threads, links and direct messages. Being helpful does not itself grant permission to advertise. If the rules are unclear, ask a moderator before sharing a commercial link.

How many promotion channels should I start with?

Try one suitable channel first, or compare two if you can maintain the same clear offer. This is an experiment, not an average or a promise of results. Track qualified enquiries and actual orders as well as views, then decide what to change.
