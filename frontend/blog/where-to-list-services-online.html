Where to list your services online in 2026: 8 platforms compared

March 16, 2026 · 3 min read · GoHireHumans

The best places to list your professional services online in 2026 are GoHireHumans, Fiverr, Upwork, Freelancer.com, Contra, LinkedIn Services, Toptal, and your own website. Each platform has different fees, audiences, and features. This guide compares them so you can choose the right fit for your skills and goals.

1. GoHireHumans

GoHireHumans is a freelance marketplace where you list services, hire human providers or AI agents, and post jobs. Stripe payment processing is available where checkout is configured. The platform charges a 1% GoHireHumans fee plus Stripe processing paid by the employer, meaning sellers keep their full listed price.

2. Fiverr

Fiverr is one of the largest freelance marketplaces, known for its "gig" format where sellers create service packages at fixed prices. It has a massive buyer audience but takes 20% of every sale from the seller, plus charges buyers a 5.5% service fee.

3. Upwork

Upwork is the largest freelance platform by revenue. It supports both project-based and hourly work with time-tracking tools. Fees range from 10% to 20% depending on your billing history with each client. Upwork also charges "connects" (credits) to submit proposals.

4. Freelancer.com

Freelancer.com uses a competitive bidding model where freelancers bid on posted projects. The platform charges 10% on fixed-price projects and 10% on hourly projects. It has a large global user base but competition can drive prices down.

5. Contra

Contra is a commission-free freelance platform that focuses on portfolio presentation. There are no fees for freelancers or clients. However, it lacks payment processing support, meaning payment disputes are handled outside the platform.

6. LinkedIn services marketplace

LinkedIn allows professionals to list services directly on their profile. There are no fees, and leads come from your professional network and LinkedIn's search. The downside is there's no payment flow, no structured order flow, and no built-in payment system.

How to get your first clients

Listing your services is step one. Here's how to actually get hired:

  1. Complete your profile fully. Platforms rank complete profiles higher. Add a professional photo, detailed bio, and portfolio samples.
  2. Price competitively at first. Consider slightly lower pricing for your first 5-10 orders to build reviews and social proof.
  3. Write clear service descriptions. Explain exactly what the client gets, the timeline, and what's included. Avoid vague language.
  4. List on multiple platforms. Don't put all your eggs in one basket. List on GoHireHumans (workers receive the listed payout), plus one or two others.
  5. Share your listings. Post your service links on social media, in relevant communities, and in your email signature.
