Best Toptal alternatives in 2026: senior talent without the markup

March 16, 2026 · 6 min read · GoHireHumans

Toptal built its reputation on connecting businesses with the "top 3% of freelance talent." But in 2026, its premium pricing model, with rates of $60 to $200+ per hour and a markup on freelancer rates that Toptal does not publish, is pushing many companies to look at alternatives.

Whether you're a startup on a tight budget, a growing business looking for profile trust signals, or an AI-forward company needing human professionals in the loop, there are now excellent platforms that deliver elite talent at a fraction of Toptal's cost.

We've compared 8 of the best Toptal alternatives for 2026, evaluating them on pricing, talent quality, verification processes, and unique features.

2. Upwork: best for largest talent pool

Upwork is the world's largest freelance marketplace with millions of professionals across every category. While fees are higher than GoHireHumans, the sheer volume of available talent makes it easy to find specialists quickly.

  • Client Fee: 5% marketplace fee
  • Freelancer Fee: 10%
  • Rates: $15–$100+/hr

Pros

  • Massive talent pool across all categories
  • Built-in time tracking and project management
  • Payment review for fixed-price contracts
  • Detailed freelancer profiles with work history

Cons

  • High freelancer fees (up to 20%)
  • Quality varies widely (requires careful vetting)
  • Complex fee structure confuses new users
  • No AI agent integration

3. Fiverr Pro: best for quick fixed-price projects

Fiverr Pro offers pre-vetted, high-end professionals with transparent fixed pricing. Ideal for well-scoped projects where you know exactly what you need: logo design, website development, or marketing campaigns.

  • Buyer Fee: 5.5%
  • Freelancer Fee: 20%
  • Rates: $50–$1,500+ per project

Pros

  • Pre-vetted professionals (Fiverr Pro tier)
  • Transparent, fixed pricing upfront
  • 500+ service categories
  • Refund policy on Pro services (check current terms)

Cons

  • 20% freelancer fee reduces talent pool quality
  • 5.5% buyer fee on top of listed price
  • Not ideal for long-term or hourly engagements
  • Race-to-bottom pricing on non-Pro tiers

4. Arc.dev: best for remote developer hiring

Arc.dev (formerly CodementorX) focuses specifically on remote software developers. They vet candidates through coding challenges and interviews, accepting only the top tier into their network. Good for teams needing embedded developers.

  • Client Fee: 15–20% markup
  • Freelancer Fee: 0%
  • Rates: $40–$120/hr

Pros

  • Rigorously vetted developers
  • Both freelance and full-time placements
  • Trial periods available
  • Focused on engineering talent

Cons

  • Limited to developers/engineers only
  • 15–20% markup adds cost
  • Smaller pool than Upwork or Fiverr

5. Turing: best for AI-matched engineering teams

Turing uses AI to match companies with pre-vetted software engineers, primarily from global talent pools. Their "Intelligent Talent Cloud" aims to reduce hiring time to days rather than weeks. Best for companies building remote engineering teams at scale.

  • Pricing: Markup included in rates
  • Rates: $30–$80/hr

Pros

  • AI-powered matching reduces hiring time
  • Lower rates through global talent sourcing
  • Full-time and part-time options
  • Managed by Turing team

Cons

  • Engineering only (no creative or marketing talent)
  • Less control over selection process
  • Markup not transparent

6. Gun.io: best for senior developers

Gun.io specializes in connecting companies with experienced, senior-level software developers. They focus on quality over quantity, with a curated network of U.S.-based and international talent.

  • Client Fee: 15–25% markup
  • Rates: $75–$175/hr

Pros

  • Senior-level developers only
  • Curated, high-quality network
  • Fast matching (48 hours)

Cons

  • Premium pricing close to Toptal
  • Small, exclusive network
  • Developers only

7. Lemon.io: best for startup-friendly rates

Lemon.io focuses on Eastern European developers, offering competitive rates for startups and growing companies. They screen candidates for English proficiency and technical skills, accepting about 4% of applicants.

  • Pricing: Markup included
  • Rates: $25–$60/hr

Pros

  • Very competitive rates for quality devs
  • 4% acceptance rate ensures quality
  • Turing advertises a first match within 24 hours
  • Replacement offered if the first match does not work out

Cons

  • Primarily Eastern European talent (timezone gaps)
  • Developers only
  • Smaller network than major platforms

8. BairesDev: best for nearshore teams

BairesDev provides nearshore software outsourcing from Latin America, offering time zone alignment with North American companies. They can provide entire teams rather than individual freelancers, making them ideal for larger projects.

  • Pricing: Markup included
  • Rates: $50–$100/hr

Pros

  • Time zone aligned with North America
  • Can provide entire development teams
  • More affordable than US-based alternatives
  • Managed project delivery option

Cons

  • Minimum engagement requirements
  • Less flexible than marketplace models
  • Pricing not fully transparent

How to choose the right Toptal alternative

The best platform for you depends on your specific needs:

  • Lowest cost: GoHireHumans (1% GoHireHumans fee plus Stripe processing) or Lemon.io ($25–$60/hr)
  • Largest talent pool: Upwork (millions of freelancers)
  • Quick fixed-price projects: Fiverr Pro
  • Senior developers only: Gun.io or Arc.dev
  • Full development teams: BairesDev or Turing
  • AI agent integration: GoHireHumans (only platform with REST API + MCP)
