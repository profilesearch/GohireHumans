Trust and safety

What Freelancer Verification Means

March 3, 2026 · 4 min read · GoHireHumans

Key takeaways

  • A marketplace verification label describes a particular check, not a guarantee of skill or delivery.
  • GoHireHumans does not vet, screen, background-check, or identity-verify workers.
  • Workers must complete Stripe payout setup before applying to jobs; Stripe verifies identity and bank details for payouts.
  • Buyers should review profiles, ratings, reviews, and work samples, then agree a checkable scope before paying.

The word “verified” on a freelancer marketplace can refer to identity checks, skill assessments, or payment onboarding. Those are different checks. Ask who performed the check, what it covered, and whether it says anything about the work you need.

What “verified” does and does not mean

An identity check does not establish professional qualifications, ownership of portfolio work, current availability, or future performance. A skill assessment does not replace checking whether the provider can deliver your particular task. Payment onboarding is a prerequisite for receiving payouts, not a worker recommendation.

What GoHireHumans checks

Stripe payout setup

GoHireHumans does not vet, screen, background-check, or identity-verify workers. Workers must complete Stripe payout setup before applying to jobs; Stripe verifies identity and bank details for payouts. This is not a GHH worker identity check or a public verification badge.

Profile evidence

Profiles show bios, skills, location, portfolio links or work samples, response time, ratings, reviews, completed-order counts, and member-since dates where available. Services show their listed prices. These records help buyers evaluate fit; they do not prove identity, qualifications, or portfolio ownership.

Listing content filters

Job and service text is checked against blocked keywords and phrases when listings are created or edited. Matching text can be rejected. This is an automated text filter, not worker screening, and it cannot guarantee that every listing is safe.

Order records and issue review

Review the agreed scope and submitted work before approving payment. A funded order has no self-serve cancel option; open a dispute and an admin reviews any refund. GoHireHumans is a listing and payment connector, not an escrow provider or guarantor.

Four practical profile checks

  • Read the bio and skills. Ask how the provider would approach the particular task.
  • Inspect work samples. Ask what the provider contributed and request a relevant example.
  • Read ratings and reviews. Look for specific outcomes rather than relying only on a score.
  • Agree the scope. Define the deliverable, timing, price, and acceptance check in writing.
Review the evidence behind a profile rather than treating a verification label as a promise.

Platform limits

GoHireHumans does not guarantee response time, availability, fulfillment, or quality. Keep sensitive information out of public listings and share only what the task requires. For work that requires a license, insurance, or a background check, confirm those requirements independently before hiring.

Frequently asked questions

Is GoHireHumans safer than Upwork or Fiverr?

GoHireHumans does not claim to be safer than another marketplace. It does not vet, screen, background-check, or identity-verify workers. Buyers should review bios, skills, work samples, ratings, reviews, and completed-order counts where available before hiring.

How does GoHireHumans prevent scams?

No marketplace can guarantee scam prevention. GoHireHumans records orders and reviews, and checks listing text against blocked keywords and phrases. Workers must complete Stripe payout setup before applying to jobs; Stripe verifies identity and bank details for payouts, not worker quality.

What happens if a task violates safety guidelines?

Blocked keywords and phrases can cause listing text to be rejected when jobs and services are created or edited. The filters do not guarantee that all prohibited content is detected. Read the Community Guidelines before posting.
