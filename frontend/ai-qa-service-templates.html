Five listing descriptions

Copy one, adjust the scope and price, and post it as a service.

AI output fact-checking

I will review AI-generated answers for incorrect, unsupported, risky, or unclear claims and return a concise correction list with source notes.

AI hallucination and source checking

I will compare AI claims against the source links or citations you provide and flag what is unsupported, misquoted, or misleading.

AI agent work review

I will audit AI-agent output before it is used in CRM, research, outreach, or operations, and return an approval checklist.

AI-built website QA testing

I will test signup, checkout, mobile layout, links, and copy on an AI-built website and return screenshots with severity notes.

AI data cleanup review

I will review spreadsheet rows, categories, duplicates, labels, and ambiguous records after an AI cleanup pass and return corrected rows with notes.

What to add before posting

A listing that states its scope and deliverable gets clearer requests.

  • A bounded unit: one document, one flow, one batch of rows
  • Turnaround in business days
  • The deliverable: the five-part report
  • What the buyer must provide: the AI output and the sources it should match
  • A price for the unit, with a note for larger scopes
