Good tasks are small and checkable

The draft you create goes into the same form employers use. These four things make it easy for a worker to say yes and for you to approve the result.

  1. Clear inputs

    URLs, files, source links, phone numbers, location, or the context a stranger would need.

  2. Clear output

    Notes, screenshots, a spreadsheet, photos, a summary, or a scorecard: name the format you want back.

  3. Clear acceptance criteria

    Checkable statements that define done, including what is out of scope.

  4. No private payment details or off-platform work

    Keep the request on the platform. Direct-payment instructions are not allowed in listings or applications.
