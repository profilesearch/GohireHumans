# International worker payouts (Connect Express)

`CONNECT_INTERNATIONAL_ENABLED` defaults to `false`: only US worker onboarding is available. Setting it to `true` exposes the curated full-service-agreement countries in `GET /payments/connect-countries` and permits country selection in `POST /payments/setup-worker`. `CONNECT_COUNTRIES_ALLOWLIST` optionally narrows that set (comma-separated country codes); it cannot expand it. A connected account's country cannot change through setup. Do not enable this flag solely because the code has shipped.

The self-serve cross-border regions for a US platform are US, UK, EEA, Canada, and Switzerland: https://docs.stripe.com/connect/cross-border-payouts. The country table omits Iceland because Stripe's US-platform onboarding selector offers a recipient agreement there rather than the full agreement. **No recipient-agreement countries are offered**: Stripe explicitly says cross-border Connect payouts cannot be made to accounts under a recipient service agreement; these would require a different approved route (such as Global payouts). Do not add India, the Philippines, Nigeria, or any other recipient country without official US-platform support and a new review. Historical recipient accounts are still evaluated for readiness, not onboarded by this list.

Non-US full-agreement Express accounts request both `card_payments` and `transfers` capabilities at creation; US accounts continue to request only `transfers`. Stripe requires both for countries such as Germany without a recipient agreement.

Before enabling, verify in the Stripe Dashboard under **Connect → Settings → Onboarding options / countries** that the platform can onboard Express accounts in each proposed country; confirm the Express configuration, transfers capability, cross-border payouts eligibility, required verification and supported payout currencies, and Stripe's account-specific requirements. Stripe lists a **0.25% cross-border payout fee** (with its stated regional exemptions). Review worker-facing pricing and local-currency disclosure. Stripe may require additional approval or restrictions beyond the static table.

Live verification plan (only after approval, without synthetic production payments): enable only approved countries in the allowlist, onboard a consented worker, inspect the created Express account country/agreement/capabilities and onboarding requirements in Stripe, verify signed `account.updated` transitions and `/payments/status`, then verify a real approved separate-charge-and-transfer payout and reconciliation. Payout readiness requires `payouts_enabled` and active `transfers`; `charges_enabled` is not required for this flow, **including existing US accounts**. Keep any test-mode evidence distinct from live approval. Roll back new international onboarding by turning `CONNECT_INTERNATIONAL_ENABLED` off; this does not reverse existing connected accounts or transfers. Do not modify Stripe or production settings as part of this PR.
